With ComPDF AI's hardware-software integration, enterprises keep their existing paper workflows. Scanners capture paper documents as digital files, ComPDF AI automatically extracts and parses them — converting invoices, contracts, reports and more into structured data assets that flow into ERP, OA and other business systems for a seamless paper-to-digital closed loop.

Core Capabilities

Four Capabilities Powering the Integration Solution

🖨️

Direct Scanner Capture

Connect directly to mainstream scanners — high-speed document scanners, flatbeds, and more — using TWAIN/WIA protocols for batch capture and auto-transfer

🤖

Intelligent Data Extraction

ComPDF AI automatically identifies key fields in scanned documents — accurately extracting amounts, dates, suppliers and other core data from invoices, contracts, reports and more

📄

Intelligent Document Parsing

Deep parsing of scanned documents with table recognition, layout analysis, and multilingual OCR — converting unstructured content into usable structured data

🔗

Seamless Business System Integration

Structured data flows automatically via API/Webhook into ERP, OA, CRM, financial systems and other existing infrastructure — no custom development required
